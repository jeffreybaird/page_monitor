Effective date: September 12, 2026

Page Monitor checks selected web-page text in your browser and notifies you when
it changes. Monitoring happens on your device. Page Monitor has no monitoring
server, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated account system.

## Information used for monitoring

Page Monitor saves the names, URLs, selected regions, intervals, durations and
status of monitors that you create. It stores the latest text snapshot, a bounded
HTML preview, and up to ten recent text changes per monitor in your browser's
local extension storage. It also stores notification delivery status and unread
counts. This information can include personal or confidential content from the
pages you choose. Only monitor content you intend to retain locally.

Unfinished forms, picker selections, progress and temporary-tab ownership are kept
in session storage. Unfinished forms are separate for each browser window and
normally last until the browser session ends. Saved monitors remain until deleted
or extension data is cleared.

The extension checks the sites you select with your browser's available session.
Requests go to those sites, which receive ordinary request information such as
your IP address and applicable cookies. The extension does not copy your site
passwords or cookies into its storage. Sites may have their own privacy practices.
When JavaScript rendering is needed, a temporary tab runs the site's own code;
that site's normal network activity can occur. The extension explains this before
the first rendering check.

Monitor URLs, selectors, snapshots and history are not sent to the developer,
ExtensionPay or Stripe. The extension does not synchronize monitor data between
browser profiles or devices.

## Optional purchases

Free monitoring does not require a payment account. If you choose to purchase or
restore the lifetime license, ExtensionPay and Stripe handle the hosted payment
and restoration process, including your email and payment information. Card
information is entered on the payment provider's pages, not in Page Monitor.

The licensing library stores a local installation date, per-install licensing
credential and provider user response, which can include email, plan and payment
status. Page Monitor separately caches its product ID and paid status. Licensing
requests send the licensing credential and retrieve purchase status from
ExtensionPay. Initial license setup can also identify a development installation.
Licensing credentials and responses are kept locally, not in browser sync storage.
Checks occur after purchase/restoration, on manual refresh, and periodically to
verify license status. No monitored page content is included in those requests.

Modern Firefox versions request optional licensing-data consent when you purchase
or restore. Revoking this consent prevents further licensing requests. Previously
verified lifetime access remains available offline unless a later successful
verification reports it as unpaid.

For the providers' practices, see [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com/) and
[Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). Deleting the extension does
not delete transaction records held independently by the payment providers.
Contact the publisher through the contact below for payment-data inquiries.

## Retention and control

Delete a monitor to remove its snapshots and history. Editing its URL, selected
region or rendering setting resets comparison/history data. Cancel a draft to
remove it. Uninstall the extension or clear its extension data to remove local
monitoring and licensing information. Restore a purchase using its original email
if you reinstall; monitor history is not restored by that process.

Local storage is not an encrypted vault. Anyone with access to your browser profile
or device may be able to access it. The extension requests site permissions as
needed and lets you remove them through your browser settings.

## Use and disclosure

Page Monitor does not sell user data or use it for advertising. Information is
used to provide monitoring, notifications and optional licensing. Use of data
obtained through browser APIs is limited to the disclosed functionality and
complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use
requirements. This policy describes the extension; third-party websites and
payment providers operate under their own policies.
